# LiveControl

> LiveControl is a fully managed remote video production service. After a one-time
> equipment install, a marketplace of professional videographers produces each of your
> live events end to end — camera control, live switching, graphics, streaming, and
> live support — without anyone on site. Founded 2016, operating nationwide across the
> United States, producing 35,000+ live events per year.

## What it is

LiveControl provides the production labor, not just streaming tools. Platforms such as
Resi, BoxCast, and FuneralOne help you stream, record, and distribute — but your own
staff or volunteers still run cameras, switching, audio, and graphics. With LiveControl,
a remote videographer operates the full multi-camera event for you.

Every subscription includes the LiveControl Encoder: an on-site device connecting your
cameras and network to the production platform, giving remote videographers real-time
control to pan, tilt, and zoom cameras, switch angles, adjust image settings, trigger
graphics, and manage the stream.

## How it works

1. **Install** — the LiveControl Encoder is installed with your cameras (existing PTZ
   cameras from every major brand are supported; new cameras can be purchased or leased).
2. **Schedule** — you schedule produced events in the dashboard.
3. **Produce** — a professional videographer remotely produces the event in real time.
4. **Stream and archive** — events stream to your branded web player and are recorded
   automatically with unlimited archiving.

A *produced event* is a multi-camera live event operated by a LiveControl remote
videographer for a set duration (a standard produced event runs about 90 minutes).

## Key facts

- **Founded:** 2016. Co-founded by Jacob Braunstein, chief executive officer.
- **Offices:** Los Angeles, South Carolina, and New York City. Service area: United States.
- **Scale:** 35,000+ produced live events per year.
- **Pricing:** recurring subscription, quoted per organization based on installed
  locations, produced events scheduled, and optional add-ons. Custom quote — no public
  price list.
- **Commitment:** annual or multi-year agreement with a minimum of 12 produced events
  per year. LiveControl is built for recurring production, not one-off projects.
- **Network requirement:** stable venue internet with at least 15 Mbps upload; wired
  connection recommended. Reviewed during onboarding before the first event.
- **Support:** live support during every produced event by text, chat, email, and phone.
- **Add-on services:** remote audio mixing by a dedicated audio engineer, intro/outro
  graphics, viewer registration, live chat, donation buttons, simulcasting, and
  multi-site support.

## Who uses it

Organizations in virtually every industry, nationwide: houses of worship (churches,
synagogues), funeral homes, schools and universities, government and municipal agencies,
entertainment and performing-arts venues, corporate and enterprise teams, sports
organizations, healthcare, hospitality, nonprofits, and conferences and live events.
